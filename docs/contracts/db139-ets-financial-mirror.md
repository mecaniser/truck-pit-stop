# DB-139 — ETS financial mirror and imported-order presentation

Status: Architecture and independent QA/Security source/test GO for the read-only display; reader-only release pending. Future-insert changes are preserved separately and held. Accountable implementation owner: Backend & Integrations. High-risk lane because invoice, payment, tenant and external accounting identities are involved. No existing financial record rewrite, QuickBooks mutation, statement send or importer execution is authorized by this document.

## Outcome and distinct states

Easy Truck Shop (ETS) remains the shop's current work and billing system while DieselBridge is the future system. The daily collector may mirror ETS facts into DieselBridge; it must not create a second customer charge or reinterpret imported history as a native DieselBridge receivable. A repair order's **work state** (`completed`) is distinct from the ETS invoice's **financial state** (`unpaid`, `partially_paid`, `paid`, or `unverified`). Completed work does not by itself prove payment or close a financial balance. For an imported order, the staff workspace must show a historical/source-owned invoice and payment state, never a native `Create Invoice` or collect-payment action. Closed/paid presentation requires source-supported settlement, not the completed work badge.

QuickBooks is a read-only reconciliation source for the current shop. It may corroborate an ETS invoice and allocated payment, but a same customer name, amount or invoice number alone is not identity proof. The existing DBN→QuickBooks outbound accounting mirror is separate from this ETS→DBN historical read model and must not be called or retried by an imported record. An ETS invoice, payment, refund, credit, void or edit arriving on a later sync is a new source observation with immutable provenance; it can supersede the displayed historical state without silently rewriting native financial events. Conflicts or missing allocations remain `unverified` and enter review.

## Source and pricing rules

For imported orders, display the exact linked ETS invoice's billed total when an unambiguous same-tenant source invoice exists. Label it `ETS invoice total`; do not recompute historical invoice value from DBN's editable labor lines, current rates, discounts or parts. Prefer a valid preserved ETS observation over stale imported financial columns. Display individual ETS labor/parts amounts only where the same source invoice has a complete, attributable breakdown. Otherwise say `Breakdown unavailable`; do not show `$0` as a proven billed labor price. If source invoice identity or amount is absent/ambiguous, display `Amount unavailable` and hold the row for review. Never display a negative DBN-calculated amount as an ETS debt, refund, overpayment or invoice total. Read-only production inspection confirmed the example PM charge is `$150`, its full invoice is `$534.41`, and QuickBooks has a directly linked payment with zero remaining balance. Private row-level evidence stays outside Git.

## Approved display extension (2026-10-18)

`GET /repair-orders/{id}/imported-billing` uses existing staff repair-order read permissions and assignment/fleet restrictions. Foreign or deleted orders return 404; a native order returns 409. Selected-shop customer context requires both tenant and customer identity in source/projection repair-order filters and legacy invoice page/count reads. Invoice/order joins include tenant equality; completed payment summaries require the selected tenant. Missing selected identity fails closed. The response contains source, optional invoice identity and total, recorded completed/remaining amounts, `payment_status` (`paid`, `partially_paid`, `unpaid`, `unverified`), `evidence_basis` (`imported_records` or `ets_source_snapshot`), `reconciliation_status=not_checked`, observation time, and review reasons. Decimal amounts serialize as strings. This endpoint has no settlement creation, provider call, commit or financial writer.

The reader requires a unique active same-tenant source invoice. A valid preserved snapshot requires digest-bound revisions, matching tenant/order/service/invoice identity, a verified complete singleton invoice manifest, trusted ETS shop, USD amounts with exact cents, complete unique allocations, supported noncancelled source payment states, and allocation sum equal to total minus source balance. Source invoice status 10 with zero balance is observed paid; status 5 with a positive balance is observed unpaid or partial according to its allocations. Unsupported statuses, credits, refunds, pending/failed payment conflicts, deleted/mixed financial evidence and source-read failures hold payment status. Observations older than 24 hours retain only the supported historical total; payment status becomes unverified. `settlement_semantics=unverified` stays unchanged: source display is not native collection eligibility or QuickBooks reconciliation.

Validated source totals/balances override stale imported columns in this read response only. Differences add a review marker. The additive `imported_invoice_total_amount` list/workspace field uses the same resolver in batched queries; unavailable source identity stays null. Native totals retain their existing path. The repair-order list hides its misleading mixed total aggregate whenever the full filtered result includes imported rows. Server `has_imported_orders` covers unloaded pages; absent metadata fails closed. Daily work queues already exclude imported orders. Without a valid snapshot, status is unverified and both paid/remaining amounts are omitted; an imported-row update time is labeled separately from a source observation.

`ETS_IMPORTED_BILLING_SHOPS` is a presentation-only tenant-to-ETS-shop JSON binding. Its default is `{}`; a missing dedicated tenant may use the existing completion mapping. Malformed or conflicting mappings hold safely. The exact Web-only release binding is prepared privately from the collector's existing verified tenant/shop configuration. It does not enable, change or restart collection, outcome delivery or QuickBooks actions.

## Proposed read contract and gates

- Tenant-scoped repair-order responses expose immutable `source` (at least `easy_truck_shop_import` versus native). An imported-order historical financial view derives from one same-tenant, noncancelled source invoice tied to that order and returns source invoice identity, observed total, allocated confirmed amount, remaining source balance when supported, status, evidence timestamp and `review_required` reason. It must distinguish source-observed balance from a DBN collectible balance.
- The native invoice creation endpoints (manual and automatic) reject imported orders before any invoice, outbox, email or QuickBooks side effect. Existing imported invoice display remains available. A cancelled or missing imported invoice does not unlock native issuance. Repeated syncs remain idempotent by source IDs and allocation IDs; source removals/edits are retained as evidence, not silently lost.
- The ETS→DBN state mapping is derived from source invoice total, balance and complete payment allocation semantics, then checked against QuickBooks invoice/payment/credit/refund records where accessible. `paid` needs a zero supported remaining balance; `partially_paid` needs supported positive allocation and positive remainder; `unpaid` needs a supported full remaining balance; unknown/mismatched values remain `unverified`. QuickBooks matching requires company/realm and shop, source invoice linkage/document reference, customer, currency, amount, and payment allocation evidence. QBO data must never overwrite native DBN settlement or cause provider writes.
- Negative cases: same amount/wrong customer or tenant; reused invoice number; split/multiple invoices; partial payment; overpayment/credit; refund/reversal; deleted/cancelled source invoice; stale source snapshot; missing QBO connection/record; QBO discrepancy; zero-priced labor with nonzero ETS invoice; historical record without invoice. Source failures must not turn a prior known paid state into a fabricated new charge or turn unknown into paid.
- Before code release: Architecture reviews the exact API/data contract and migration compatibility; focused backend/frontend tests plus isolated source fixtures; independent Security and QA review; protected CI; exact merge/deploy; fixed-cohort financial and ETS-capture retention; signed-in Light/Night desktop/mobile verification. No bulk historical financial rewrite or QuickBooks apply without a separate reviewed, exact write set and specific approval.
