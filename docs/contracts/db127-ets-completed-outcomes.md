# DB-127: ETS completed invoice evidence

Backend & Integrations owns this high-risk producer change. Existing source observations remain immutable, and imported repairs remain `easy_truck_shop_import`. No payment row, settlement time, manual qualification, invoice amount, attribution link or historical hold is rewritten.

The collector preserves the explicit ETS completed flag, status, source UTC completion time, authenticated public shop identity and observation time. Shop identity requires the authenticated user's internal shop to equal the service's shop. A full invoice-index enumeration uses the source-supported `sort=number` ordering and must have `status=all`, `range=all`, empty search, stable page count and total, unique invoice identities, and matching service relationships. One embedded invoice is not a complete manifest. Any missing, repeated, filtered, cross-shop or conflicting row holds the manifest. All invoice IDs, including unsupported/cancelled invoices, participate in completeness. Explicit standalone `SALE` rows with no service count toward global coverage but are excluded from repair manifests; unknown missing service relationships fail closed.

The adapter accepts only completed=true, service status60, invoice status10, USD positive invoice totals with zero balances, and complete positive status20 allocations with verified payment type codes0/10 and provider codes0/1 (no guessed meanings), explicit canceled=false and zero credit/unapplied amounts. Decimal allocation sums must equal each invoice total. It never sums a payment's full total across invoices or uses payment.date as settlement or conversion time. Conversion time is the source completion instant. Imported invoice UUIDs remain aliases; canonical revenue identity is the source shop plus ETS service ID.

The existing bounded history sweep can produce immutable signed `repair.completed_invoice_snapshot` and `repair.completed_invoice_hold` records. Both share one per-service observation sequence and tenant transaction lock. Known operation siblings share the same canonical invoice root; conflicting roots/customer/vehicle identities hold. A later invalid observation produces a diagnostic hold, and source observations older than24hours cannot restore positive eligibility. Payload erasure applies the existing retention policy. Only the actual current source observation timestamp refreshes evidence; another local scan cannot make it fresh.

The feature defaults disabled with `ETS_COMPLETED_OUTCOMES_ENABLED=false` and an empty `ETS_COMPLETED_OUTCOME_SHOPS` mapping. Activation requires an independently reviewed exact tenant-to-public-shop mapping, complete-source capture, negative-observation delivery and receiver freshness verification. Existing signing transport is reused; no new credential, account, Google destination or campaign change is required. Deployment alone does not activate this path.
