# DB-135 customer receivables contract (B1)

Status: Discovery contract; no financial writer, provider call, statement delivery, or collection action is authorized by this item. Accountable owner: Backend & Integrations. Architecture & API Contracts owns this cross-layer contract before frontend/backend implementation. High-risk lane: financial identity, tenant isolation, and sensitive customer account data.

## User outcome and source boundary

Staff can open one customer account and review invoices, confirmed payments, credit activity, current balance, and invoice-level aging; download a clearly dated statement for review. The five aging buckets are current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90+ days overdue. An invoice is aged by its **remaining principal**, not its status text. Imported Easy Truck Shop (ETS) history is shown separately and is never added to DieselBridge's native collectible total or aged as a native receivable.

The [ETS receivables guide](https://support.easytruckshop.io/en/articles/16739469-who-owes-me-money-getting-your-a-r-and-building-an-aging-report) exports invoice balance and due date to a spreadsheet for aging; [customer transactions](https://support.easytruckshop.io/en/articles/16416336-where-to-view-customer-transactions-customer-transactions) includes invoices, payments, credits and refunds. Its [balance explanation](https://support.easytruckshop.io/en/articles/16738694-why-doesn-t-my-customer-s-balance-match-their-invoices) warns that starting balances and unapplied credits can differ from invoice sums. Native aging is a DieselBridge addition, not an ETS feature claim.

Current source: `Invoice` has immutable `billed_customer_id` at issue, nullable on older rows; `RepairOrder.customer_id` can change on merge. `InvoiceSettlement` carries principal/confirmed principal; `Payment` is invoice-bound; `CustomerCreditEntry` is append-only. The existing customer-list `balance` and `CustomerReadModel.invoice_total - payment_total` mix source ownership, use current RO customer identity, and are not an authoritative native collectible amount. B1 must introduce a separate read model/API; do not silently relabel that legacy field.

## Read contract

For a native invoice supported by a time-limited reviewed receivable attestation, the invoice row reports `reconciliation_basis: reviewed_attestation`; a trusted native settlement reports `native_settlement`. The `native` summary reports `reviewed_attestation_count`. The account and PDF label reviewed historical receivables as such and distinguish them from payment-provider receipts. The attestation must pass the separate [DB-135 attestation contract](db135-receivable-attestation.md) within the same read-only snapshot. Any settlement row, even one that fails trust, prevents attestation fallback; an untrusted settlement leaves the invoice in review. An internal repair order or fleet customer also prevents either basis from becoming collectible.

`GET /api/v1/customers/{customer_id}/account` returns a current snapshot with `as_of_date` in `Tenant.timezone`, `generated_at`, `currency: USD`, `native` (`collectible`, `collectible_complete`, `unverified_invoice_count`, five bucket totals, `undated_due`, `unapplied_credit`, `review_required_count`), `ets_history` (`observed_open_balance`, `review_required_count`, `collectible: false`), and a bounded, newest-first transaction page. If any eligible native invoice has untrusted settlement or unresolved bill-to identity, `collectible_complete` is false and the numeric `collectible` and aging buckets are **verified subtotals only**, never a complete account balance; UI and PDF must prominently say the balance is incomplete and must not be used to collect. Pending payments and credit audit exceptions alone do not change this invoice completeness flag. Each row has stable `id`, `kind` (invoice/payment/credit/refund), `source_owner` (native/ets_history/review_required), `occurred_at` or date-only source date, `date_basis`, `invoice_id` and invoice number when applicable, amount, current remaining principal only for invoices, and a drill-down route. Credit rows expose their action and whether they increase, decrease, or do not separately change available credit. `limit` is 1–100 (default 50); opaque signed keyset cursor orders by `(occurred_at, kind, id)` and is bound to tenant/customer/filter. A new row inserted before the cursor does not repeat or skip existing later rows. Invalid cursor/filter is 422; absent or cross-tenant customer is 404; unauthorized role is 403. Requests exceeding the bounded account read (5,000 invoices, 10,000 payments or 10,000 credits) return 413 instead of silently omitting financial rows. No provider identifiers, bank references, card details, private notes or source secrets appear.

`GET /api/v1/customers/{customer_id}/account/statement.pdf` returns a downloadable, server-rendered **current account snapshot**, with generation timestamp, shop-local as-of date, customer identity, source-separated totals, invoice balances, payment and credit activity, and an explicit `ETS historical — not a DieselBridge collection balance` section. It is a read-only preview/download; there is no send button, notification, persisted statement issuance, or historical backdated-balance claim. The response is `application/pdf`, `Cache-Control: private, no-store`, with a bounded output size and transaction count. If the printable activity cap is reached, the PDF states the number of omitted rows and that displayed rows do not reconcile to the full-account totals; it must never present the truncated table as complete. A future period-statement feature needs its own historical opening-balance contract; filtering activity must never relabel today's balance as a prior-date balance.

Only authenticated garage owners and garage admins with the existing `payments` permission may use these account-wide financial routes. Receptionists, customer/driver/fleet roles, cross-tenant IDs and forged cursors have negative tests. Every query and child join constrains `tenant_id`; the requested customer must be active in the actor's tenant. The service performs no writes, commits, external calls, credit application, invoice status updates, or reminders. Each request uses one `REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY` database transaction so totals and rows share a consistent committed snapshot; a refresh may see newer payments.

## Accounting and identity rules

1. Native collectible invoices are nondeleted, noninternal, finalized (sent/paid/overdue), noncancelled, nonvoided, and neither `source='easy_truck_shop_import'` nor `accounting_policy='historical_export_hold'`. ETS source and historical holds belong only in historical/review sections. Drafts, superseded revisions and internal fleet cost records cannot become receivables.
2. For an eligible native invoice with a verified settlement, `remaining = max(principal_total - confirmed_principal, 0)`, in Decimal cents. Pending attempts never reduce it; confirmed principal already includes applied credit and reversals/refunds handled by the settlement writer. Show the underlying invoice total and settled amount for reconciliation. Never sum `Payment.amount` **again** beside `confirmed_principal`. A trusted settlement has status `native` or `reconciled`, same-tenant invoice/customer lineage, nonnegative confirmed principal no greater than principal, and `principal_total` matching the principal from `invoice_money_snapshot` to the cent. `reconciled` additionally requires evidence that no unsupported paid baseline contributed to confirmed principal. `legacy_paid_snapshot`, `legacy_paid_without_tender`, `legacy_overage_review_required`, `backfill_running`, unknown statuses, and a ledger event for a paid baseline without reconstructed tender are never trusted merely because the invoice says PAID.
3. An eligible native invoice without a trusted settlement, or with conflicting principal/tenant/customer lineage, is `review_required`; do not invent a collectible balance or place it in an aging bucket. The GET path must not call lazy settlement creation, which can synthesize paid principal from invoice status without tender evidence. Before release, quantify coverage on a fixed production cohort and address material gaps through a separately reviewed reconciliation/backfill, not an implicit GET-side mutation.
4. Native unapplied credit follows the existing origin-bound eligibility rule: sum each issued credit less only its direct `applied`, `refunded`, and `reversed` children, clamped at zero. A reversal linked to an application is audit history, not a second deduction from the issued origin. Preserve consent/overpayment state checks before calling it available. It is displayed separately; only an actual applied entry reflected in confirmed invoice principal reduces an invoice. Overpayments/refund-pending states are not automatically available credit. No credit is auto-applied or used to offset aging.
5. Imported ETS invoice/payment rows retain `source_owner=ets_history`. Current imported rows do not carry an authoritative ETS opening balance or invoice-level outstanding balance, so `ets_history.observed_open_balance` is `null` with `balance_basis: unavailable`; it is never calculated as imported invoices minus imported payments. Transaction amounts may be shown as imported activity, not as source-confirmed debt. Do not infer ETS starting balances, source transaction dates or debt ownership from import timestamps. Mixed native/ETS payment lineage or unsupported financial events are `review_required`, never silently moved into native totals.
6. Bill-to identity uses `Invoice.billed_customer_id` when it resolves to an active same-tenant customer; only a null pre-155 snapshot falls back to the same-tenant RO customer. The existing merge writer moves ROs and deletes the loser but does not update invoice/settlement customer snapshots or save a merge map. A nonnull snapshot that no longer resolves is therefore never silently attributed to the RO's current customer or counted as collectible. For an active account linked by that RO, show only a masked `review_required` exception (invoice ID and reason, no amount or customer history) to an authorized staff user; the exception is excluded from totals and aging until identity is reconciled under a separately reviewed merge/backfill contract. A nondeleted eligible invoice on a soft-deleted RO is also a masked review exception for its bill-to account (or the null-snapshot legacy RO customer), even if it has a settlement; it makes the verified subtotal incomplete. Count tenant-wide unresolved invoices in release coverage so orphaned rows are not lost. Test null legacy snapshots, deleted/merged nonnull snapshots, soft-deleted ROs, and a RO later assigned to an unrelated customer.
7. Convert `due_date` to `Tenant.timezone` date. `days_overdue = max(0, as_of_date - due_date)`; day 0 and future dates are current. Missing due date is `undated_due`, separate from current. Bucket totals include only positive remaining native principal; exactly 30/31/60/61/90/91 days must be tested. Fully settled invoices appear in activity but add zero to aging.

## Acceptance and release gates

- One customer account shows source-separated totals and drillable invoices, confirmed payments, credit entries/refunds; statement PDF matches the same snapshot and is downloadable only, never sent.
- Desktop and mobile Shop in Light and Night preserve a single scroll region, readable numbers, cancel/back/return context, loading/empty/failure/retry states and no wrapped primary controls.
- Contract tests cover decimal cents, partial payments, credit application without double count, pending/failed/refunded payment, void/cancel/draft/internal, historical hold, ETS import, missing due date, bucket edges, merged identity, tenant/role denial, pagination and PDF escaping/size bounds. Fixed-cohort financial rows remain unchanged by reads and deployment.
- Independent QA and Security review a final implementation candidate. Protected PR CI, merge, normal Web/Worker health as applicable, authenticated production desktop/mobile acceptance, and a source-separated fixed-cohort retention receipt precede Done. No real invoice, payment, credit, statement send or provider action is created solely for QA.

Compatibility: additive read routes and UI plus the separately reviewed, additive migration 179 attestation tables; existing balance projections and payment writers remain unchanged. A further schema/index/merge resolution discovered during implementation returns to Architecture, updates this contract, and gets a migration/rollback gate before frontend/backend proceed.
