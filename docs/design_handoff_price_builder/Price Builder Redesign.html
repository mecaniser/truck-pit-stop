Price Builder · UX redesign

One list, not a stack of boxes

A repair order is really just a list of work performed — each operation carrying its labor and its parts. The current drawer wraps every one of those in its own bordered card, nesting three deep. The redesign keeps all the complexity — hourly diagnostics, repair operations, per-part customer-vs-stock pricing, discounts — but presents it as a single, scannable line-item list with progressive disclosure.

1

Merge the three add-boxes

Diagnostics, Repair Operation and Services & Labor become one line-item list with a single smart add bar.

2

Collapse by default

Each operation is a one-line summary; expand only the one you're editing. A 6-line order fits on screen.

3

Hairlines, not nested cards

Dividers + indentation replace box-in-box. The drawer is the only card.

4

Anchored price editing

Customer vs stock price opens in a proper anchored popover with margin — never floating over the table.

5

Sticky totals footer

A persistent, scannable breakdown + primary action. Discounts live in one popover, not a cramped row.

6

Context in the header

Customer & truck surface up top where they belong, not buried below the builder.
